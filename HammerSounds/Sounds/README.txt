Hammer sound files go here (.ogg or .mp3 only).

To add your own: drop the file here, add its name to the SOUNDS list at
the top of HammerSounds.lua, then fully restart WoW (/reload won't see
new sound files). If you upload to CurseForge, only include sounds you
have the rights to share (CC0 / public domain or your own).

squeak.ogg is an original sound made for this addon.
